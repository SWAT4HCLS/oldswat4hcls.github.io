The new paper submission deadline is now Wednesday December 6.

]]> Informal hands-on workshop on materials for FAIRification training, organised through the EJP RD https://www.swat4ls.org/news/leiden-2024/informal-hands-on-workshop-on-materials-for-fairification-training-organised-through-the-ejp-rd/ Thu, 13 Jan 2022 14:20:41 +0000 http://www.swat4ls.org/?p=3652 Continue reading →]]> Colleagues from the European Joint Programme Rare Diseases have organised a workshop following the official SWAT4HCLS conference to work on material for FAIRification training (typically also applicable to FAIRification projects in general). The workshop is intended as an open, hands-on meeting for identifying and working on new materials and updating existing materials. Topics to work on will be selected by the participants of the workshop and could include training workflows and technical tools that for FAIRification steps. The primary users of the materials are rare disease data managers, but materials are usually more general than that.

The workshop is kindly supported by the European Joint Programme Rare Diseases (https://www.ejprarediseases.org/) and partly facilitated by the Semantic Web Applications for Health Care and Life Sciences conference 2021-22 (SWAT4HCLS 2021-22, http://swat4ls.org/).

For more information and call details see https://sites.google.com/view/material4fairification/home
