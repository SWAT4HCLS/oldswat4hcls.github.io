The workshop was held in Edinburgh, and hosted by the UK National e-Science Center (NeSC).

The e-Science Institute is less than 15 minutes walk from Waverley rail station and from St Andrews square bus stations. All information that is needed to reach Edinburgh and the NeSC is available on-line in NeSC web site.

Information for reaching the location of the workshop is available on-line at the Travel to the e-Science Institute & NeSC page.

Information for reaching Edinburgh is also available on-line. See the Travel to and from Edinburgh & Glasgow page.
